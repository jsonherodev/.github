**A free online JSON viewer, formatter, validator and editor that runs in your browser.**<br>
Format, minify, validate and search JSON with no sign-up and no ads. Your JSON stays on your device unless you click Share.

### [Open the editor →](https://jsonhero.dev/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=org-profile)

- **Format and validate** JSON, with best-effort formatting when the input is broken
- **Search, fold and minify** large documents, then download the result
- **Extract JSON from logs** and turn escaped JSON strings back into readable JSON
- **Share a short link** when you need to, kept for 7 days on the free plan or 30 days on Pro
